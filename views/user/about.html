About MUNify

What is MUN?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an educational simulation in which students can learn about diplomacy, international relations, and the United Nations. At an MUN conference, students work as the representative of a country, organization, or person, and must solve a problem with other delegates from around the world.

What is MUNify?

MUNify is a comprehensive digital platform designed to modernize the Model United Nations experience. We provide tools for committee management, real-time voting, automated scoring, and secure certificate issuance, ensuring that conferences run smoothly and fairly.

Benefits for Students

Participating in MUN simulations helps students develop critical thinking, public speaking, research, and negotiation skills. MUNify enhances this by providing a professional environment where students can focus on diplomacy while our technology handles the logistics.
